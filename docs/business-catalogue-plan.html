A catalogue that plays like Road to Rich

Every prestige level gets 8–9 businesses, each of one of three kinds: fleets (buy vehicles: delivery, car rental, taxis, trucks, planes), site chains (open restaurants, stations, hotels and level each one up) and equipment businesses (one place you fill with machines). Money keeps counting every second, like RichLife today. Growth is slower, and the game gives reasons to come back several times a day. No code yet — this page is for you to check first.

1. Three kinds of business

🚚 FleetBuy vehicles

Delivery, car rental, taxis, buses, trucks, ships, planes. A depot per city holds the vehicles (upgrade it for more spaces). Each vehicle earns per second, breaks down on its own, and is repaired from the business page. Like Road to Rich: real models and prices, from a Honda PCX to a Boeing 777F.

🏪 Site chainOpen sites, level them up

Restaurants, gas stations, hotels, clinics, malls. You open sites of different formats (Burger Joint, Pizzeria, Sushi Bar, Steakhouse), each on its own street, and level each one 1 → 10: every level adds +25 % of the site's income and costs more than the last (Road to Rich's rule).

🧰 EquipmentOne place, fill it with machines

Laundromat, gym, vending, solar, data centres. One place (or a few) you fill with machines that each earn and can break — the model already shipped in spec 014.

All three keep what exists: business level (×2 at 10/25/50), a manager for offline income (4-hour shifts — and managers repair), taxes, the store boost.

2. Slower growth — the numbers

Today a $3,000 car-wash bay pays for itself in about 17 minutes, so players race through prestige in a day. The new catalogue is built from one rule: how long a purchase takes to pay for itself, longer at each prestige.

3. Reasons to come back (engagement)

⏱ Manager shifts

Already there: 4-hour shifts. Rehire to keep earning offline.

🔧 Breakdowns

Already there: broken vehicles and machines earn nothing until fixed.

🎁 Daily reward new

Log in each day for diamonds: 5, 5, 10, 10, 15, 15, 30 on day 7, then again. Missing a day restarts the week.

📦 Daily contracts new

Three small goals a day ("own 3 delivery vehicles", "upgrade a restaurant", "earn $50K"): each pays cash + diamonds. New ones every day at midnight UTC.

Contracts and daily reward are phase 2; the catalogue and rules are phase 1.

5. Rule changes behind it

  • Site formats: a place kind gets its own price and income (a Steakhouse earns more than a Burger Joint). Today every place earns the business's base income × its area.
  • Site levels become Road to Rich's: +25 % of the site's income per level (was +5 %), level n → n+1 costs 25 % of the site's price × 1.35^(n−1). Capacity still grows per level.
  • Kind on each business (Fleet, Sites, Equipment) so the dialog speaks the right words ("Vehicles · Depots", "Restaurants", "Machines") and shows the right card.
  • Everything else — breakdowns, repairs, managers repairing, taxes, business levels — stays as shipped in 014.
  • All of it is content in the database, editable in the admin catalogue editor (kind and site incomes added there).

6. Decisions I need from you

1. Existing players.

Businesses already bought keep the (higher) numbers they were bought with. On Netlify that means early testers stay far ahead. Keep them, or reset every player's game (diamonds, badges and avatars kept, like the admin reset)?

My pick: reset all players once when this ships — the economy changes too much to mix.

2. Pace.

Is the §2 table the right difficulty (≈ 2–3 h to P2, a day to P3, about two months to P7 for an active player)?

My pick: yes; every number is in the database, so we can tune it later from the admin panel.

3. Vehicle wear like Road to Rich.

Road to Rich also has mileage, services every N km, a 1,000,000 km limit and a used-vehicle lot. Add them now, or keep vehicles to "break and repair" for this release?

My pick: later — breakdowns already give vehicles a life; mileage is a second release.

4. Engagement phase.

Daily reward and daily contracts (§3): ship them with this, or right after?

My pick: right after, as phase 2, once the new economy is in.
