Can RichLife succeed — and how a solo dev gets it known

Short answer: yes in Tunisia and the Maghreb, as a niche hit, if it feels local and is easy to share. Worldwide it is hard — idle tycoons are a crowded genre (AdVenture Capitalist, Idle Miner Tycoon) — but web game portals give a solo dev real reach for free. The game itself is solid; what is missing is retention tools, sharing and distribution, not more businesses.

Where it stands

Strengths

  • English, French and Arabic (RTL) — rare for this genre
  • Plays in the browser: no install, works on any phone
  • Real cars, planes and brands — easy to understand and show
  • Deep loop already: places, levels, breakdowns, managers, bank, taxes, prestige, store
  • Leaderboard with countries — a ready-made rivalry hook

What blocks growth today

  • Free server sleeps: a new player waits 30–60 s on the first visit and leaves
  • Must register before playing; no tutorial
  • No notifications — idle games live on "your manager finished" pings
  • Nothing to share, no invites, no reason to tell a friend
  • No income and no analytics (you cannot see who stays)
  • Generic name: "RichLife" is hard to find in search and stores

What to build before marketing (in order)

  1. Server that never sleeps must — Render paid (~$7/month) or similar. First impression is everything.
  2. Play as guest, register later to save must — remove the wall before the fun.
  3. 60-second tutorial: open a food cart, buy a unit, hire a manager must
  4. Install as an app (PWA) + push notifications — "shift ended", "3 units broken", "daily reward ready" must
  5. Daily reward + daily contracts (phase 2 of spec 015) soon
  6. Invite a friend: both get diamonds when the friend reaches P2 soon
  7. Share cards: one tap makes an image "I reached Billionaire in 9 days 🇹🇳" for WhatsApp/Instagram stories soon
  8. Analytics (PostHog or Plausible, free tier): D1 / D7 retention, where players quit soon
  9. Money: rewarded ads for diamonds (watch an ad → 5 💎), later the Play Store through a TWA/Capacitor wrapper soon

Making it viral in Tunisia

  • Local businesses next to the global ones: lablabi stand, louage line, harissa factory, olive-oil press, Djerba resort, Sousse hotel. Tunisians share what looks like them.
  • Country battles: weekly "Tunisia vs Algeria vs Morocco vs France" leaderboard — national pride spreads by itself.
  • Seasons: a 4-week race with a badge for the top 100 — a reason to start, and to come back.
  • Darija in videos and posts, even if the game stays EN/FR/AR.

Numbers to watch

  • D1 retention ≥ 35 %, D7 ≥ 12 % — below that, fix the game before buying traffic.
  • Invites per player ≥ 0.3 — the start of organic growth.
  • Cost: ~$7–20/month hosting, ~$12/year domain, $50–150 for a first influencer and ads test.

Solo-dev rules

  • One feature a week, one video a week — consistency beats bursts.
  • Talk to 10 real players before each big feature; build what they ask for twice.
  • Don't spend on ads until D7 retention is good — paid players leave as fast as they come.
